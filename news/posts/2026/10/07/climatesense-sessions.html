About ClimateSense

ClimateSense is a European research project on climate misinformation. We study where false and misleading climate claims appear, when they come back and how they spread across countries.

We build tools for the people who deal with these claims. The aim is to help fact-checkers decide what to check first and to give policymakers clearer evidence.

The project runs from March 2025 to February 2028. It is coordinated by The Open University (United Kingdom), with Kaunas University of Technology (Lithuania), EURECOM (France) and Prague University of Economics and Business (Czech Republic).
